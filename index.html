01 / Current research interests

My research centers on hybrid entrepreneurship, with a broader interest in how experiences across multiple jobs shape people's lives and careers.

FOCUS / 01
Multiple jobholding Entrepreneurship

Hybrid
entrepreneurship.

What happens when someone combines paid employment with an entrepreneurial venture? I am interested in how these overlapping work experiences shape individual well-being and meaningful career development.

A question I explore

How can entrepreneurial experiences alongside a primary job open up new possibilities for a person's working life?

FOCUS / 02 Across work roles

Positive spillover.
Across roles.

I explore how experiences in one work role can positively influence experiences in another.

A question I explore

When and how do experiences across multiple jobs enrich one another, creating opportunities for both individuals and organizations?

FOCUS / 03 Well-being & careers

Working well.
Finding meaning.

I am interested in how holding multiple jobs shapes individual well-being and the development of meaningful careers.

A question I explore

Under what conditions can multiple work roles support a fulfilling working life and a meaningful career path?

02 / About Seohyeon

One person.
Many possibilities.

Seohyeon is a master’s student at the SNU OP Lab. She received her bachelor's degree in Psychology from Chung-Ang University with magna cum laude distinction.

Her research interest focuses on how experiences across multiple jobs influence individual well-being and meaningful career development, with particular emphasis on positive spillover between multiple work roles.
